---
title: "Not getting notifications?"
description: "What to check when symetric's check-in reminders aren't arriving on your iPhone or Android phone."
label: "Help"
---

symetric's check-ins arrive at different times during the hours you chose, so the reminder is the only thing telling you one is due. If reminders aren't arriving, it's almost always one of the settings below on your phone, not something wrong with your account.

To test, open symetric and go to **Settings › Notifications › Notification test › Send**. A notification should arrive within a few seconds.

## On an iPhone

1. **Notifications are allowed.** Open **Settings › symetric › Notifications**. Allow Notifications should be on, with Banners or Lock Screen ticked.
2. **A Focus isn't hiding them.** Do Not Disturb, Sleep and Work Focus silence any app that isn't on their allowed list. Add symetric to the list, or try the test again with Focus off.
3. **They aren't held for a summary.** Open **Settings › Notifications › Scheduled Summary**. If symetric is in it, reminders wait for the summary instead of arriving on time.

## On an Android phone

1. **Notifications are allowed.** Open **Settings › Apps › symetric › Notifications**. They should be on, including the reminders category.
2. **Battery saving isn't stopping symetric.** Open **Settings › Apps › symetric › Battery** and choose **Unrestricted** (on some phones, "Don't optimise"). Samsung, Xiaomi, OnePlus and Oppo phones put apps to sleep to save battery, which is the usual reason reminders work for a day or two and then stop.
3. **Do Not Disturb isn't on.** Swipe down from the top of the screen and check that Do Not Disturb, or a bedtime or focus mode, is off.

## Still not working?

Turn **Push notifications** off and on again in symetric's Settings. That registers your phone afresh, which fixes a reminder setup that has gone stale.

If that doesn't do it, [get in touch](/support) and tell us your phone model and what the test said. We'll help you sort it out.
