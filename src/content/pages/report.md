The report is the whole point. Everything else, the check-ins, the domains you picked, the quiet weeks, exists to build towards five pages you can actually hand someone.

This sample is built from three months of made-up data, not a real person's, so you can see exactly what the report looks like and decide if it's worth your time before you install anything. [Download it directly](/symetric-sample-report.pdf). No email address, no account, no sign-up.

**Page 1, Executive summary.** How many check-ins, over what date range, and a baseline-vs-current read on every domain you tracked, mind and body. The firmest patterns detected in the window sit here too, each labelled by how much evidence it rests on, along with anything you'd flagged to bring up with a clinician.

**Page 2, Mind overview.** Sleep for the period, a sparkline for each mind domain against your own baseline, and a timeline of events and interventions, therapy starts, referrals, medication changes, laid over the same weeks so a shift and its likely cause sit side by side.

**Page 3, Body overview.** The same sparkline treatment for anything you logged under body, plus notable events (a joint giving way, a migraine, a crash), and a tally of where pain or instability showed up most.

**Page 4, Context &amp; connections.** Time-of-day patterns, like symptoms that are consistently worse in the morning, and relationships between domains, like two things that reliably move in opposite directions. Every relationship here is a qualitative direction and a confidence tier, not a raw correlation coefficient, so it isn't read with more authority than self-reported data warrants.

**Page 5, Data quality &amp; methodology.** A week-by-week look at how consistently you checked in, with any low-coverage stretch called out so findings from that period are read with appropriate caution, followed by a plain explanation of how baselines, patterns and thresholds are calculated.

## What it isn't

The report describes what you logged. It doesn't diagnose anything, and it doesn't tell you or your clinician what to do next. <b>It's a record, not a recommendation.</b>
