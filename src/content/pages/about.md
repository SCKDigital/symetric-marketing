symetric started in February 2026 because I knew there was more going on with me than just “anxiety”. But all of my mental and physical symptoms felt too vague to describe properly to a clinician during a fifteen minute appointment.

So I built the app I needed. Not just another mood tracker, but something that could measure these aspects of my mind and body that I knew were related and would form a more complete picture if I could just get the data. Turn my subjective experience into something more objective. I’m not an app developer so I used AI to help build it, and iterate on it over and over again before it went on the app store in August 2026. [Read exactly how AI was used](/how-ai-was-used).

I’ve used symetric myself, every day, since. **That’s my story so far: one person, one very long streak of real use, and a handful of other alpha testers. No clinical trial (yet!).** I’m also writing a proper case study for clinicians, more rigorous and more technical, which will live on [the clinicians page](/for-clinicians) once it’s ready. This page isn’t that, it's just to give you the background on why the app exists.
