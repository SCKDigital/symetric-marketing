## 1. About us and this policy

<p class="in-short">In short: symetric is an Australian symptom-tracking app, and because you're entering health information, we hold it to the highest standard of protection Australian privacy law has.</p>

symetric is operated by SCK Digital, ABN 79 120 082 906 ("we", "us", "our"). This policy explains how we handle personal information, including health information, when you use the symetric mobile app.

symetric is a personal symptom and mood tracking app. It helps you record how you're feeling over time, identifies patterns in your own data, and helps you prepare for appointments with your doctor.

We treat the information you enter into symetric as **health information** under the *Privacy Act 1988* (Cth), which is a type of **sensitive information** and attracts the highest level of protection under the Australian Privacy Principles. As we operate from Victoria, the *Health Records Act 2001* (Vic) and its Health Privacy Principles also apply to how we handle your health information. We handle it accordingly.

**symetric is currently available to users in Australia only.**

## 2. Your consent

<p class="in-short">In short: We ask for your consent in separate, specific steps when you first use the app, not as one blanket tick box, and you can take it back any time by deleting your account or turning off body or cycle tracking in Settings.</p>

Because the information you enter is health information, we need your consent to collect it, and we ask for it in separate, specific steps rather than one blanket agreement.

After you verify your email, before any health information is collected, we confirm you're 18 or over, then ask you to consent to mind-domain tracking (the areas like mood and anxiety you choose to track). Body tracking and cycle tracking are optional and asked about separately again, each with its own consent, so you're only ever asked to consent to what you've actually said you want to use.

You can withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account (see section 10), which withdraws all of it. You can also turn body tracking or cycle tracking off individually at any time in Settings, without deleting your account or affecting your mind-tracking consent.

You do not have to use symetric, and you do not have to enter any particular piece of information beyond what's needed for the consents above. Every field is optional except your email address.

## 3. What we collect

<p class="in-short">In short: Your account details, the health data you choose to enter, and the patterns we generate from it, and a specific list of things we deliberately don't touch.</p>

### Account information
- Email address (symetric doesn't use passwords, we email you a one-time link to sign in instead)
- Your date of birth, to confirm you're 18 or over
- Your timezone
- Your notification preferences, including any do-not-disturb hours you set

### Health information you enter
- **Daily scores** across eight areas: mood, energy, anxiety, concentration, irritability, social battery, sensory sensitivity, and motivation
- **Baseline scores** you provide when you first set up the app
- **Check-in details**: when you completed each check-in, whether you used quick or full mode, whether you used the late-completion window, and whether you later edited an entry (including the number of edits and when they were made)
- **Sleep**: a daily sleep score and whether you felt rested
- **Treatment and life context**: medication events you record (starting, stopping, increasing or decreasing a medication `[TODO: confirm whether the medication name itself is captured if entered]`), therapy sessions, and significant life events
- **Appointment information**: appointment dates, the questions you prepare, which patterns you mark for discussion, and any notes you record after the appointment

### Information we generate about you
symetric analyses your entries to produce new information about you, including detected patterns, correlations between different areas, changes in your personal baseline, cluster classifications, and confidence ratings for each pattern.

**This generated information is also health information about you**, and we protect it the same way.

### Technical information
- Device push notification tokens issued by Apple or Google, so we can send you check-in reminders
- Device information such as operating system version, app version, device model and device identifiers
- IP address
- App usage events collected through our analytics provider (see section 6)
- Information stored locally on your device, including cached data and scheduling information

### What we do **not** collect
We want to be specific about this, because it matters for an app like ours. symetric does **not**:
- Access your location
- Access your camera, photos, microphone or contacts
- Connect to Apple Health, Google Fit, or any wearable device
- Send your health information to any artificial intelligence or large language model service
- Sell your information, or use it for advertising or marketing by any third party
- Use your health information for any purpose other than providing the app to you

<p class="in-short">In short: To run the app, find patterns in your data, remind you to check in, and generate your reports, never for marketing, advertising or research.</p>

We use your information to:

- Meet our legal obligations

## 7. How your information is analysed

<p class="in-short">In short: Pattern detection happens on your device. Your scores aren't sent anywhere to be analysed by us or anyone else.</p>

All pattern detection in symetric runs **on your device**. Your scores are not sent to a server to be analysed, and no third party performs analysis on your data.

Your entries are, however, stored in our database so they are available across sessions and so you don't lose them if you change devices. We want to be clear about the distinction: the analysis is local, the storage is not.

## 8. Our access to your information

<p class="in-short">In short: Named staff can see your entries only for support, technical or security reasons, and every access is logged.</p>

Authorised personnel can access the information you enter into symetric, including your scores and entries.

We access identifiable information only where necessary, to respond to a support request you've made, to investigate a technical fault, or to protect the security of the service. Access is limited to named individuals, and access to production data is logged.

We think you should know this rather than assume otherwise.

## 9. Security

<p class="in-short">In short: Encryption, per-account database isolation, restricted access, and logging.</p>

We protect your information using:
- Encryption in transit and at rest
- Row-level security in our database, so your records are only accessible to your account
- Access controls limiting which personnel can reach production data
- Logging of administrative access

No system is completely secure. If a data breach occurs that is likely to cause you serious harm, we will notify you and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner as required under the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme.
